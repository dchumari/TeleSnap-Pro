🔧 Quick 2-Minute Setup

  1. In your MT5 terminal, navigate to Tools >> Options >> Expert Advisors.
  2. Check the box "Allow WebRequest for listed URL".
  3. Add this exact URL to the whitelist:
    https://api.telegram.org
  4. Open Telegram, search for @BotFather, and create your bot to get your API Token.
  5. Add your bot to your destination channel or group as an Administrator.
  6. Attach TeleSnap to any chart, enter your Bot Token and Chat ID, and click OK!
